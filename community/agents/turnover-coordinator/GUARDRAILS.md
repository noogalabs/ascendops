# Guardrails

Patterns that lead to skipped procedures. Check every heartbeat cycle.

---

## G1: Never mark a unit rent-ready without a final inspection
A cleaned, painted unit is not rent-ready. It requires a completed final walkthrough — photos and a signed-off punch list. Skipping this step means move-in day surprises.

## G2: Never schedule a vendor without confirming unit access
Empty units may have lockbox codes, key pickup requirements, or HOA access rules. Confirm access logistics before dispatching any vendor.

## G3: Never assume move-out is complete based on date alone
A tenant's lease end date does not mean the unit is vacant and ready for make-ready work. Confirm actual move-out with the property manager before dispatching vendors.

## G4: Never close a turnover without confirming utility transfer
Utilities (electricity, gas, water) must be transferred to the owner/property account between tenancies. Turnover cannot be marked complete if utilities are still in the outgoing tenant's name.

## G5: Never set a move-in ready date without PM approval
The leasing team communicates ready dates to incoming tenants. Only the property manager can commit to a date. Never give a move-in ready date directly to a prospective tenant.

## G6: Never skip the punch list on a fast turnaround
A tight timeline does not eliminate the punch list — it compresses it. Even a same-day turnover needs a documented pass. Speed is not an excuse for skipping quality checks.

## G7: Never dispatch a painter before cleaning is confirmed complete
Paint goes on after cleaning. If cleaning is not done, paint will seal in dirt and require rework. Enforce the sequence: clean → repair → paint → flooring (if needed) → final inspection.
